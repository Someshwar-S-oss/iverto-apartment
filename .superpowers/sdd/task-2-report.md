# Task 2 Report: Official PDF Receipt Generator Service & Endpoint

## Summary of Changes
1. **Receipt PDF Streaming in `BillingReportsService`**:
   - Implemented `streamInvoiceReceiptPdf(societyId: string, invoiceId: string, outStream: NodeJS.WritableStream): Promise<void>` in `backend/src/modules/billing/billing-reports.service.ts`.
   - Injected `InvoicesService` into `BillingReportsService`.
   - Queries invoice detail via `invoicesService.getDetail(societyId, invoiceId)`.
   - Fetches society metadata (name, address), unit & building details (`units.unitNumber`, `buildings.name`), and cycle period label.
   - Built a publication-grade, professional A4 PDF invoice/receipt using `pdfkit`:
     - **Header**: Society Name (bold 18pt), Society Address, "OFFICIAL MAINTENANCE BILL & PAYMENT RECEIPT", Generated Date, and divider.
     - **Invoice & Unit Details Block**: 2-column styled container with Invoice Number, Billing Period, Due Date, Unit/Flat display, and color-coded Status badge (`PAID`, `PARTIALLY PAID`, `OVERDUE`, `PENDING`).
     - **Itemized Charges Table**: Table header (Description, Category, Amount in ₹), item rows with currency formatting, and Total Invoiced Amount line.
     - **Payment History Section**: Included whenever payments exist, detailing Date, Method (`RAZORPAY`, `MANUAL`, `OFFLINE`), Reference/Txn ID, Payer identity & role (`paidByName` and `paidByRole`), and Amount Paid in ₹.
     - **Financial Summary Box**: Clean summary box displaying Total Invoiced, Total Amount Paid, and Outstanding Balance.
     - **Footer**: Standard disclaimer note ("This is a computer-generated receipt/invoice for society maintenance dues. Generated by Iverto Apartment Management.").
     - Streams to `outStream` and resolves upon completion.

2. **Mobile/Resident Controller Endpoint**:
   - In `backend/src/controllers/mobile/mobile-billing.controller.ts`:
     - Injected `BillingReportsService`.
     - Added `GET /api/v1/mobile/units/:unitId/billing/invoices/:id/receipt` protected by `@RequirePermission('billing.view', ScopeType.UNIT)`.
     - Validates that the invoice belongs to the requested `unitId`, throwing `NotFoundException` if mismatched or if unit is missing.
     - Sets headers `Content-Type: application/pdf` and `Content-Disposition: inline; filename="receipt-${invoice.invoiceNumber}.pdf"`.
     - Delegates PDF streaming to `billingReportsService.streamInvoiceReceiptPdf(societyId, id, res)`.
